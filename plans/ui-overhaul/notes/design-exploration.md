# Direction exploration

Planning only. No direction contract, implementation approval, final token system
or production DESIGN.md is claimed.

## Grounded systems considered

1. **Aegean navigation and passage:** indigo/limestone, readable geographic hierarchy,
   journeys expressed through original landscape art. Strong daylight alternative.
2. **The painted stoa:** civic clarity, painted architectural color and restrained
   headings; standard work areas within a confident structural palette.
3. **Red-figure pottery:** dark ground and limited terracotta narrative vignettes;
   strong identity but more difficult for state-rich everyday controls.
4. **Hellenistic relief:** controlled highlights and deliberate depth; risk of
   sculptural decoration competing with data and action clarity.
5. **Epic manuscript:** ink, readable writing measure and margin rhythm; naturally
   strong for the journal but too narrow to govern every working surface alone.
6. **Roman civic inscription:** concise hierarchy, aligned labels and durable
   structure; risk of too much all-caps display type in a daily app.
7. **Spartan woven mantle:** oxblood cloth, ivory and bronze, an ordered rhythm and
   a single weighted commitment-card transition. This became Spartan Standard.

The seven cover graphic/cartographic, ceramic, architectural/sculptural,
manuscript and textile traditions. Their controls remain familiar web controls.

The Impeccable exploration used direction seed `a8584c0c` in Operate mode and
assigned grounded candidate 7. The explicit Greek/Roman/Spartan brief constrains
every candidate. The seed is an exploration device, not a recorded user preference.

## Challenger evaluation

Assessed only against audience identification and product clarity. The final
world remains an unapproved proposal for the user's return.

- **Botanical folio:** declined as a replacement world. Its strongest discipline
  is keeping comparable views at a consistent scale; carry that into activity
  legends and the six-dimension illustration family, without adopting botanical UI.
- **Full-viewport vertical media feed:** competitive for one-at-a-time focus,
  unsuitable as the app-wide navigation model. Keep focused card hierarchy,
  but explicit Done/Skip and navigation remain standard controls.
- **Warm consumer app:** competitive on clarity and touch comfort; it does not
  express the pinned epic identity as strongly. Raise the proposal to its target
  sizes, visible labels and clear action contrast without borrowing its theme.
- **Airline ticket wallet:** declined as a world; its strongest discipline is a
  legible distinction between pending and recorded action. Apply that to saving
  and failed-response states; do not add tickets, tears or VOID stamps.
- **Labanotation score:** declined as a world; learn its consistent temporal
  encoding for activity history. Do not adopt its staff, unfamiliar navigation
  or movement symbols for a productivity app.
- **Variable-font specimen:** declined as a world; its strongest discipline is
  type fidelity and alignment. Keep a controlled type scale and avoid ornamental
  typography in working controls; do not make font manipulation part of the UI.

Spartan Standard and Aegean Passage are shown concretely. The category-standard
accessible productivity layout remains available if the user prefers to reduce
the theme; this is a standing alternative, not the recommendation.

## Open direction choices

Dark/light emphasis, degree of heroic or mythic figures, and comp-first versus
code-first implementation remain unapproved. The first concept is a proposed
working baseline, not a substitute for the user's final choice.
