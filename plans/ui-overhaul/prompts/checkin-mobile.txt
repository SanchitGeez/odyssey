Use case: ui-mockup. Asset type: high fidelity portrait mobile product screen concept, 9:19 aspect ratio, edge to edge with no phone or browser frame.
Create Odyssey's daily check-in screen in the Spartan Standard design language: smoked teal-charcoal surfaces, oxblood header accents, warm ivory type and brushed bronze primary actions. Sophisticated modern personal productivity app enriched by ancient Greek/Iliad atmosphere, never a game HUD. Readable mobile controls with generous thumb targets and normal-case sans-serif labels; restrained inscription serif only on the app name and card title.
Top: compact Odyssey wordmark, Today date navigation with left/right buttons, an Account icon with accessible menu intention. Under it a clearly labeled progress indicator '2 of 5 complete'. Main focus: one large beautiful tactile card showing a small panoramic ancient Aegean coastal path image in its top third, category Vitality, task title '10-minute walk', schedule 'Daily', supporting line 'A small commitment to your health.' One next-card edge behind it shows it belongs to a sequence. Card can be dragged horizontally; show subtle unobtrusive directional cue 'Swipe left to skip' and 'Swipe right for done', but keep visible large Skip and Done buttons below. An optional 'Log a value' disclosure below the buttons. Bottom: stable five-destination navigation Check-In (active), Tasks, Quests, Journal, Insights. All content fits the tall mobile viewport with no horizontal overflow. Main card and actions sit in the reachable middle/lower area.
Art and surfaces feel like dyed woven fabric, Aegean stone and bronze through tiny details only, with a crisp professional grid. The primary surface remains smooth and readable. 16px+ mobile text, 44-48px minimum action targets. No tiny uppercase controls, no neon, shimmer, radial glows, glassmorphism, medieval symbols, XP, scores, rewards, gore or armored videogame character. No fake cursor and no giant statues in the work area. Sample state for a proposed app, not an implemented screen.
